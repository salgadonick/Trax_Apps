Trax Apps

Operator and bench apps for Trax machines. They connect over Bluetooth from the browser; nothing to install.

GSE tiller handle Tugs with the Trax tiller-handle logic board. Recognises any Trax unit from its ID and shows the matching dashboard.

Which browser

  • Android: Chrome.
  • iPhone / iPad: Bluefy from the App Store (Safari has no Web Bluetooth).
  • Desktop: Chrome or Edge.
  • Add the app to your home screen from the browser menu for a full-screen app with the Trax icon.
